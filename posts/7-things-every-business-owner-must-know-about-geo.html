7 things every business owner must know about GEO.

AI is now the front door to search. If your business isn't built to be cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews, you're invisible to a fast-growing slice of your buyers. Here's the plain-English playbook.

Search just changed shape — and most businesses haven't noticed

For 20 years, search meant a list of blue links. You optimized to rank #1, the user clicked, you won. That model is breaking. Today, more users ask an AI a full question and get a synthesized answer — no list, no click, just one recommendation.

That recommendation either includes your business or it doesn't. There is no second page.

02

GEO is not SEO with a new name

SEO optimizes for an algorithm that ranks pages. GEO optimizes for a model that reads, reasons, and quotes. The difference matters: AI doesn't care about your keyword density, it cares whether your content gives a clear, structured, trustworthy answer it can lift into its reply.

Traditional SEO
  • Ranks pages on a results list
  • Rewards keywords + backlinks
  • Click drives the outcome
  • Updates over months
GEO
  • Cited inside the AI's answer
  • Rewards structure + clarity + trust
  • Recommendation drives the outcome
  • Updates in days to weeks
If SEO was "be on page one," GEO is "be the sentence the AI says out loud."

05

Structure beats wordcount

AI models extract answers, not paragraphs. Posts that use clear headings, short answer blocks, lists, tables, and schema get cited at roughly 4× the rate of long, undifferentiated walls of text — even when the wordcount is identical.

Practically: lead each section with the answer. Use one idea per paragraph. Add an FAQ with FAQPage schema (this post does). Wrap stats and definitions in obvious blocks the model can lift.

06

Trust signals do most of the work

AI engines won't quote sources they don't trust. The signals that matter most: a real author with a real byline, an "About" page that explains who you are and what you do, transparent contact info, and external mentions on third-party sites the model already trusts (industry directories, reviews, press).

07

The window is small — and the leaders are compounding

Here's the part nobody likes to hear. AI engines build durable preferences for the sources they cite often. Every month you wait, your competitors get cited more, become "the trusted answer" in your category, and harder to dislodge.

Right now, in most industries, the AI doesn't have a clear favorite yet. That window closes fast. The businesses that move in 2026 own the citations of 2027.

GEO isn't a marketing channel. It's a land grab.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?
GEO is the practice of optimizing your content so AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini cite, summarize, and recommend your business when users ask buying-intent questions.
Is GEO replacing SEO?
No — GEO sits on top of SEO. You still need a crawlable, fast, well-structured site. GEO adds a new layer: structuring content so AI models can extract, trust, and quote it directly inside their answers.
How long does GEO take to show results?
Most businesses start seeing citations in AI answers within 4 to 8 weeks of implementing GEO fundamentals — much faster than traditional SEO because AI models update their knowledge continuously.
Do I need GEO if my business is local?
Yes — possibly more than national brands. Local buyers increasingly ask AI assistants "best X near me" and "who should I hire for Y in [city]". If you're not structured for AI to pull, your competitors will be the recommendation.
What's the first thing I should do to start GEO?
Audit how AI engines currently describe your business. Run your top 10 buying-intent queries through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The gap between what they say and what you want them to say is your GEO roadmap.
