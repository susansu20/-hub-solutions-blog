The three platform options every Singapore SME should consider

There are dozens of website platforms, but only three categories matter for most small businesses in Singapore: WordPress, Shopify, and custom-built solutions. Each serves a different business model and budget.

WordPress powers 43% of all websites globally — and for good reason. It's flexible, SEO-friendly, and you own everything. Best for service businesses, agencies, content-driven sites, and businesses that need full control over design and integrations.

Shopify is the leading e-commerce platform. If your business model is primarily online sales, Shopify handles product listings, payments, inventory, and shipping out of the box. It's less flexible than WordPress, but faster to launch for pure e-commerce.

Custom platforms (built from scratch) make sense only when you need highly specific functionality — think SaaS products, membership platforms, or complex booking systems. They're expensive, time-consuming, and harder to maintain, but give you total control.

For 80% of Singapore SMEs, WordPress is the right answer. For product-based businesses, Shopify. Custom builds are rarely worth it unless you're venture-backed.
03

WordPress vs. Shopify — the comparison that matters

Most Singapore business owners narrow their decision to WordPress or Shopify. Here's the honest breakdown of what each platform does well — and where it falls short.

Shopify
  • Built for e-commerce — products, payments, shipping all integrated
  • Faster initial setup for online stores
  • Monthly subscription model (SGD 40–400/month)
  • Less design flexibility, harder to customize
  • SEO is good but not as powerful as WordPress
  • You don't own the platform — Shopify does
WordPress
  • Flexible — works for any business type, from blogs to booking systems
  • Best-in-class SEO control and optimization
  • You own the site, the content, and the data
  • Thousands of plugins for any feature you need
  • Hosting is separate (more control, but requires choosing a good host)
  • Slightly steeper learning curve, but far more powerful long-term

Hosting matters more than most business owners realize

You can build a beautiful website on the best platform — and still lose customers if your hosting is slow, unreliable, or insecure. Hosting is the foundation. It controls your site's speed, uptime, security, and how well it handles traffic spikes.

Many Singapore SMEs choose the cheapest shared hosting option (SGD 5–10/month) and then wonder why their site is slow, goes down during peak hours, or gets hacked. Cheap hosting is a false economy — the cost of lost customers, downtime, and security breaches far exceeds the savings.

The old way vs. the new way of choosing a platform

Ten years ago, choosing a website platform meant picking between expensive custom builds or clunky DIY tools. Today, the market has matured — but many Singapore business owners still make decisions based on outdated advice.

The old way
  • Pick the cheapest option to save money upfront
  • Use whatever the nephew or freelancer recommends
  • Assume all hosting is the same
  • Build once, never update or optimize
  • Ignore mobile performance and SEO
The new way
  • Choose based on long-term business goals, not upfront cost
  • Match platform to business model (e-commerce = Shopify, services = WordPress)
  • Invest in quality hosting from day one
  • Build with SEO, speed, and mobile performance as priorities
  • Plan for growth — scalability, integrations, and future features

SEO starts with your platform and hosting choice

Google's ranking algorithm prioritizes fast, secure, mobile-friendly websites. If your platform or hosting can't deliver those fundamentals, you're fighting an uphill SEO battle from day one.

WordPress gives you the most SEO control — clean URLs, meta tags, schema markup, image optimization, and plugin support for advanced features. Shopify's SEO is good, but more constrained. Custom platforms can be optimized well, but require developer expertise for every change.

Hosting impacts SEO directly through site speed. Google uses Core Web Vitals (load time, interactivity, visual stability) as ranking factors. Slow hosting = poor Core Web Vitals = lower rankings. Managed hosting solves this.

We've seen Singapore SMEs jump 15–40 positions in Google rankings just by migrating from cheap shared hosting to managed WordPress hosting. Same site, same content — just faster infrastructure.

The real cost of a small business website in Singapore

Budget is always a factor, but understanding the total cost of ownership prevents expensive mistakes. A cheap website that doesn't convert, loads slowly, or needs constant fixes ends up costing far more than a well-built site from the start.

WordPress site: Professional design and build typically costs SGD 3,000–8,000. Hosting costs SGD 30–150/month. Annual maintenance (updates, backups, security) runs SGD 600–1,800/year. Total year-one cost: SGD 4,000–11,000.

Shopify site: Theme customization and setup costs SGD 2,500–7,000. Shopify subscription is SGD 40–400/month. Apps and integrations add SGD 20–150/month. Total year-one cost: SGD 3,500–10,000.

Custom platform: Development starts at SGD 15,000 and commonly exceeds SGD 40,000. Ongoing maintenance requires a developer on retainer (SGD 2,000–6,000/month). Custom builds make sense only for unique business models or venture-backed startups.

Reality check: A professionally built WordPress or Shopify site costs less than most Singapore SMEs spend on a single trade show booth — but delivers 24/7 marketing, lead generation, and customer acquisition for years.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the best website platform for a small business in Singapore?
For most Singapore SMEs, WordPress paired with managed hosting offers the best balance of flexibility, cost, and long-term control. Shopify is ideal if you're primarily e-commerce. Custom platforms make sense only when you need highly specific functionality that off-the-shelf solutions can't deliver.
Should I use WordPress or Shopify for my business website?
Use Shopify if selling products is your primary function and you want an all-in-one e-commerce solution. Use WordPress if you need a content-rich site, service business site, or want full control over SEO, design, and integrations. WordPress is more flexible; Shopify is more streamlined for online stores.
Does hosting really matter for a small business website?
Yes — hosting determines your site's speed, uptime, and security. Cheap shared hosting often leads to slow load times, frequent downtime, and poor support. Managed WordPress hosting or a reputable cloud host (like Cloudways or WP Engine) delivers faster performance, better security, and measurably higher conversion rates.
How much does a good small business website cost in Singapore?
A professionally built WordPress or Shopify site for a Singapore SME typically ranges from SGD 3,000 to SGD 12,000 depending on complexity, design, and integrations. Ongoing hosting and maintenance typically costs SGD 50–300 per month. DIY platforms can start cheaper but often lack the polish, speed, and SEO optimization that drive real business results.
Can I build my own website or should I hire a professional?
You can build your own site using platforms like WordPress, Shopify, or Wix — but professional web design delivers measurably better results. A well-built site loads faster, ranks higher on Google, converts more visitors, and saves you hundreds of hours. For most Singapore business owners, hiring a web design agency is the faster, more profitable path.
